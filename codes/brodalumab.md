## Supporting Facts

- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Brodalumab achieved a 44% PASI 100 response rate after 12 weeks, compared with 22% for a leading comparator.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside.11.9-22.FINAL_-1.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** In a second study, 37% of patients receiving brodalumab achieved PASI 100, compared with 19% for the comparator.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside.11.9-22.FINAL_-1.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** 86% of patients treated with brodalumab achieved a PASI 75 response.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside.11.9-22.FINAL_-1.pdf>_
- **[Financial Condition]** The Phase 3 clinical trial of brodalumab was funded by Amgen and AstraZeneca.
