## Rationale by Dimension

### Clinical Uniqueness (+5.0)

Mount Sinai’s PCI program shows markedly lower risk‑adjusted mortality (0.76% vs 1.15% statewide) and non‑emergency mortality (0.45% vs 0.74% statewide), and is the only high‑volume New York State hospital with such superior outcomes, indicating a highly unique, high‑quality service that Cigna would likely need to keep in‑network.
