- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** daraxonrasib is a pancreatic cancer drug that doubled median survival from 6.5 to 13 months in metastatic patients in a phase 3 trial.  
  _source: <https://med.stanford.edu/news/insights/2026/06/pancreatic-cancer-drug-daraxonrasib-what-to-know.html>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** daraxonrasib is an oral pill with fewer toxic side effects than standard chemotherapy, with common side effects severe rashes and mouth sores.  
  _source: <https://med.stanford.edu/news/insights/2026/06/pancreatic-cancer-drug-daraxonrasib-what-to-know.html>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** daraxonrasib works by tethering the K‑Ras protein to another protein, blocking its active regions and inhibiting any mutated K‑Ras.  
  _source: <https://med.stanford.edu/news/insights/2026/06/pancreatic-cancer-drug-daraxonrasib-what-to-know.html>_
- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** daraxonrasib received expanded access from the FDA, allowing certain metastatic pancreatic cancer patients to receive it before approval.
