## Supporting Facts

- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** The EndoBarrier device is a thin, flexible tube‑shaped liner designed to stay in place for one year.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Mount-Sinai_82472-Inside-v2.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** The EndoBarrier is guided through the mouth and into the stomach, where it is placed at the beginning of the small intestine.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Mount-Sinai_82472-Inside-v2.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Evidence to date suggests that there is continued control of blood sugar even after the EndoBarrier device has been removed.
