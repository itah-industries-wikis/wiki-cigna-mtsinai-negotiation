## Supporting Facts

- **[Financial Condition]** Sixty percent of each APC payment is attributed to employee wage costs and is adjusted by local wage indices.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Financial Condition]** Hospitals that meet quality reporting requirements receive a 2.6% increase in APC payments for 2026.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** APCs apply to outpatient surgery, outpatient clinics, emergency department, observation services, outpatient testing, and therapies including certain drugs, intravenous infusion therapies, and blood products.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Drugs and supplies with small associated cost are included in APC payments, while expensive medications such as chemotherapy may be paid separately.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Durable medical equipment is paid for through a non-APC methodology.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Financial Condition]** The Rural Emergency Hospital designation, effective January 1, 2023, provides supplemental payment for certain services covered by APCs.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** APCs are not paid for most diagnostic radiology studies, add-on codes such as IV infusions, and IV push dose medications.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** APCs bundle outpatient testing and therapies, but add-on codes may be paid separately.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Financial Condition]** APC payments are made only to hospitals when the Medicare outpatient is discharged from the ED or clinic or transferred to another facility not affiliated with the initial hospital.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
- **[Financial Condition]** APC payments are fixed prospective payments, placing hospitals at risk for profit or loss on each APC.  
  _source: <https://www.acep.org/administration/reimbursement/reimbursement-faqs/apc-ambulatory-payment-classifications-faq/>_
