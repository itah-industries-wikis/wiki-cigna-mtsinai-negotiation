## Supporting Facts

- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** The Inspire sleep apnea device was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in 2014.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside.11.9-22.FINAL_-1.pdf>_
- **[Financial Condition]** The Inspire device surgery takes between two to three hours under general anesthesia on an outpatient basis.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside.11.9-22.FINAL_-1.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** The Inspire device is the first relatively predictable surgical alternative for properly selected patients with sleep apnea.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside.11.9-22.FINAL_-1.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** After Inspire implantation, the median number of obstructed breathing events per hour dropped by 68%, from 29.3 to 9.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside.11.9-22.FINAL_-1.pdf>_
- **[Financial Condition]** Patients implanted with Inspire return once a year for device checks, providing ongoing revenue opportunities.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside.11.9-22.FINAL_-1.pdf>_
