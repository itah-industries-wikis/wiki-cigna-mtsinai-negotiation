## Rationale by Dimension

### Financial Condition (-2.0)

Mount Sinai’s extensive use of J1815 (multiple NDCs, high billing units) indicates a heavy reliance on insulin volume, suggesting they may need payer volume to sustain revenue, giving Cigna some leverage.

### Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks (-3.0)

CMS benchmark rates and HCPCS amount data for J1815 provide Cigna with a clear reference point to negotiate lower rates, favoring Cigna.

### Clinical Uniqueness (-4.0)

Insulin injections are a commodity drug with many alternative providers; Mount Sinai is not a unique or specialty provider for J1815, so Cigna can steer patients elsewhere.
