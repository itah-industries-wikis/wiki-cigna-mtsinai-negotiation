## Rationale by Dimension

### Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks (-3.0)

CMS data shows J1817 has a fixed HCPCS amount of 2 per unit and has been continuously covered since 2003, giving Cigna a clear benchmark to negotiate lower rates.

### Clinical Uniqueness (-2.0)

J1817 covers specialized insulin pump products (e.g., Humalog MDV, Humulin R U‑500) used at Mount Sinai, but insulin pumps are widely available at many providers, so the service is not uniquely tied to Mount Sinai.
