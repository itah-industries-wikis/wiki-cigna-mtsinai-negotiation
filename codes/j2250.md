- **[Financial Condition]** HCPCS code J2250 corresponds to midazolam 1 mg IV, with billing units 1 mg, reimbursement amount 1, effective from 01/27/2006.

- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** HCPCS J2250 for midazolam injection 1 mg had a reimbursement amount of 101 per unit from 01/27/2006 onward.  
  _source: <https://www.dmepdac.com/palmetto/PDAC_files.nsf/F/PDAC2022-04-05Xwalk.pdf/$FILE/2022-04-05Xwalk.pdf>_
- **[unclassified]** J2250 is the HCPCS code for midazolam hydrochloride injection 1 mg, effective from 01/27/2006.
