### Clinical Uniqueness (+4.0)

JAG201 is a one‑time brain infusion trial launched exclusively at Mount Sinai’s Seaver Autism Center, and it is the first study of its kind for Phelan‑McDermid syndrome, indicating a highly specialized, non‑commodity service that Mount Sinai uniquely offers.

## Supporting Facts

- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** The JAG201 trial launched in February 2025 at the Seaver Autism Center at Mount Sinai, and three children have received the infusion so far.  
  _source: <https://reports.mountsinai.org/article/psych2026-03-phelan-mcdermid-autism-gene-therapy>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** JAG201 is a one-time infusion into the brain, injected into the lateral ventricle.  
  _source: <https://reports.mountsinai.org/article/psych2026-03-phelan-mcdermid-autism-gene-therapy>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** JAG201 contains a miniaturized version of the SHANK3 gene where core functional binding sites are conserved.  
  _source: <https://reports.mountsinai.org/article/psych2026-03-phelan-mcdermid-autism-gene-therapy>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Early safety data for JAG201 have been encouraging.
