## Supporting Facts

- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** LMU Klinikum offers Lecanemab (Leqembi) as a newly approved amyloid‑antibody therapy for early‑stage Alzheimer disease.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Financial Condition]** Lecanemab therapy can reduce Alzheimer disease progression by approximately one‑third in early‑stage patients, potentially lowering long‑term care costs.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Financial Condition]** Lecanemab therapy can be discontinued early if amyloid PET shows sufficient reduction, saving treatment costs.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** Lecanemab (Leqembi) received FDA and EMA approval for early‑stage Alzheimer disease, establishing a benchmark for pricing and reimbursement.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Lecanemab is administered as an intravenous infusion every two or four weeks for up to 18 months in early‑stage Alzheimer patients.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Lecanemab therapy requires regular MRI monitoring for amyloid‑related imaging abnormalities (ARIA) to manage potential side effects.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Market / Network Position]** LMU Klinikum requires referral by a primary or neurologist for Lecanemab therapy, indicating a controlled network for access.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Lecanemab therapy is only indicated for patients with mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia in early Alzheimer disease, not for moderate or severe stages.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Lecanemab therapy is contraindicated in patients with active anticoagulation, cerebral amyloid angiopathy, or ApoE4 homozygosity.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Lecanemab therapy requires comprehensive diagnostics including neuropsychological testing, imaging, and genetic testing before initiation.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
- **[Financial Condition]** The use of amyloid PET imaging to guide Lecanemab therapy can reduce unnecessary treatment duration and associated costs.  
  _source: <https://www.lmu-klinikum.de/newscenter/patientengeschichte-neue-behandlungsmoglichkeiten-bei-alzheimer/40e4ba10817c97a0>_
