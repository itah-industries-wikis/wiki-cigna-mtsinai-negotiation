## Supporting Facts

- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** Lipfendra (enlicitide) was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in July 2026.  
  _source: <https://health.mountsinai.org/blog/what-are-the-benefits-of-the-new-cholesterol-medicine-and-could-it-help-me/>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Lipfendra is an oral daily pill that can be taken with a statin, unlike other PCSK9 inhibitors that require injections.  
  _source: <https://health.mountsinai.org/blog/what-are-the-benefits-of-the-new-cholesterol-medicine-and-could-it-help-me/>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Clinical studies show Lipfendra reduces LDL cholesterol by 57% to 59%.  
  _source: <https://health.mountsinai.org/blog/what-are-the-benefits-of-the-new-cholesterol-medicine-and-could-it-help-me/>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Lipfendra is particularly effective for patients with genetic hypercholesterolemia.
