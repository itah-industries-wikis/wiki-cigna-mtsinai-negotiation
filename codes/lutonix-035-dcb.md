### Clinical Uniqueness (+4.0)

Mount Sinai was the first U.S. institution to use a drug‑coated balloon for lower‑extremity revascularization and the study demonstrates superior outcomes, indicating a high level of clinical uniqueness that favors Mount Sinai.

## Supporting Facts

- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** The Lutonix® 035 Drug-Coated Balloon (DCB) Catheter, manufactured by C. R. Bard, Inc., was approved in October to treat arteries above the knee narrowed or blocked by peripheral arterial disease.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/012615-inside.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** The drug-coated balloon study results demonstrated improved blood flow, increased patient mobility for walking longer distances, and reduced rates of blood clots.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/012615-inside.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Mount Sinai was the first institution in the United States to use a drug-coated balloon to reopen arteries in a patient’s leg.
