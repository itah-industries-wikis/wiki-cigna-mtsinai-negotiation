## Supporting Facts

- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** The telemedicine trial tracks patients’ adherence to the diabetes drug metformin, which is being repurposed to keep prostate cancer in check.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Mount-Sinai_84738-Inside.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** In a study of 1,916 pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma patients with pre-existing diabetes, metformin use was associated with a 12% decreased risk of mortality (HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.81-0.96, P<0.01) compared to other hypoglycemic medications.  
  _source: <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/27430290>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Mean survival for patients on metformin was 5.5 months versus 4.2 months for those not on metformin (P<0.01).  
  _source: <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/27430290>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** 57.3% (1,098 of 1,916) of the studied patients were treated with metformin.  
  _source: <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/27430290>_
