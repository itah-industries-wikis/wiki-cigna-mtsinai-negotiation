## Rationale by Dimension

### Clinical Uniqueness (-3.0)

Orilissa is a specialty drug approved for endometriosis pain and uterine fibroids, but it can be prescribed by many providers, not just Mount Sinai, giving Cigna leverage.

## Supporting Facts

- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** Orilissa (elagolix) received FDA approval in July 2018 for treatment of pain associated with endometriosis.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/10/Inside_Oct-8-21_2018_FOR-BLOG.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Orilissa is the first oral regimen that specifically helps to ease moderate to severe pain associated with endometriosis.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/10/Inside_Oct-8-21_2018_FOR-BLOG.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Orilissa has been shown to be helpful in the treatment of uterine fibroids.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/10/Inside_Oct-8-21_2018_FOR-BLOG.pdf>_
- **[unclassified]** Orilissa was released by AbbVie in cooperation with Neurocrine Biosciences.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/10/Inside_Oct-8-21_2018_FOR-BLOG.pdf>_
- **[unclassified]** Orilissa's generic name is elagolix.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/10/Inside_Oct-8-21_2018_FOR-BLOG.pdf>_
