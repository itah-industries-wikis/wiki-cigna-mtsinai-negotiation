## Rationale by Dimension

### Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks (+2.0)

PHIL has not yet been approved by the FDA and was used only under a single compassionate‑use petition, so there are no CMS benchmark rates or price‑transparency data to give Cigna a reference point to negotiate lower prices, favoring Mount Sinai.

### Clinical Uniqueness (+3.0)

Mount Sinai was the first institution in the nation to use PHIL for treating an arteriovenous fistula under FDA compassionate use, indicating a unique, specialized service that is not widely available elsewhere.

## Supporting Facts

- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Mount Sinai was the first institution in the nation to use PHIL, a liquid embolic super‑glue substance, for treating an arteriovenous fistula under FDA compassionate use.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/09/Inside_9-10-9-23_2018-Blog-1.pdf>_
- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** PHIL has not yet been approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, but Mount Sinai petitioned the FDA for compassionate use for a single patient.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/09/Inside_9-10-9-23_2018-Blog-1.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** PHIL appears grey on X‑ray due to iodine, allowing clear visualization during embolization procedures.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/09/Inside_9-10-9-23_2018-Blog-1.pdf>_
