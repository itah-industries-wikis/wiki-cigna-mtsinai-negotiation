## Rationale by Dimension

### Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks (-3.0)

Probuphine’s FDA approval as the first implantable buprenorphine device provides a clear regulatory benchmark that Cigna can use to negotiate lower rates, favoring Cigna.

### Clinical Uniqueness (+4.0)

Probuphine offers unique benefits—six‑month sustained delivery, reduced relapse, elimination of accidental use and non‑adherence—making it a specialty service that Mount Sinai can position as a must‑have provider, favoring Mount Sinai.

## Supporting Facts

- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** Probuphine is the first FDA-approved implantable buprenorphine device for opioid addiction.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside_Sept-12-25_2016-FINAL.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Probuphine delivers low-level doses of buprenorphine for six months after insertion under the skin in the upper arm.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside_Sept-12-25_2016-FINAL.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Probuphine consists of four one-inch-long rods.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside_Sept-12-25_2016-FINAL.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** In a clinical trial, 177 participants at 21 sites showed that Probuphine users did not relapse compared to oral buprenorphine users.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside_Sept-12-25_2016-FINAL.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Probuphine eliminates accidental use by children.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside_Sept-12-25_2016-FINAL.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Probuphine eliminates the frequent problem of non-adherence among opioid addicts.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside_Sept-12-25_2016-FINAL.pdf>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Probuphine is recommended as part of a comprehensive treatment plan that includes counseling and psychosocial support.  
  _source: <https://inside.mountsinai.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2018/03/Inside_Sept-12-25_2016-FINAL.pdf>_
