## Rationale by Dimension

### Clinical Uniqueness (-2.0)

The Vitamin K shot is a standard, preventative care item administered at most birthing facilities, suggesting it is a commodity service. This weakens Mount Sinai's unique negotiating position, despite the critical nature of the service.

## Supporting Facts

- **[Regulatory & Pricing Benchmarks]** The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention reports that newborns who do not receive the vitamin K shot are 81 times more likely than those who do to develop late vitamin K deficiency bleeding.  
  _source: <https://www.propublica.org/article/vitamin-k-baby-autopsy-reports>_
- **[Clinical Uniqueness]** Autopsy reports may list the cause of death as 'hemorrhagic disease of newborn,' which is the previous name for vitamin K deficiency bleeding.
